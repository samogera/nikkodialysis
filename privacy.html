Privacy policy

Nikko Dialysis Center ("we") is the data controller for information collected through this website, registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) under no. [NO.]. Contact our data protection officer at info@example.com.

What we collect

Only what we need to respond to you: your name, phone number, optional email, the services you ask about, preferred contact method and time, and anything you choose to type in the message box. We do not ask for medical records through this website.

Health information

Information about your health is sensitive personal data. We process it only with your explicit consent, given on our booking form, or where needed to provide your care, and we keep it confidential under professional medical duties.

How we use it

To call or message you back, arrange consultations and sessions, check your SHA or insurance cover with your permission, and, only if you opt in, send patient education updates. We never sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Your insurer or SHA (to verify cover, with your consent), your referring or home dialysis unit (for transfers and holiday sessions), and service providers who host our website and email under written data-processing agreements. Where data is stored outside Kenya, we apply the safeguards the Act requires.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to care are deleted after 12 months. Clinical records are kept for the period required by Kenyan health regulations.

Security

This site uses HTTPS encryption. Enquiries are stored with access limited to staff who need them, and backed up securely.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can:

  • be told how your data is used
  • access the data we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to or withdraw consent for processing, including marketing, at any time
  • complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke)

To use any of these rights, email info@example.com or ask at reception. If you are in the EU or UK, you have equivalent rights under the GDPR.

Patient rights

You have the right to respectful, safe care; to clear information about your treatment and costs; to a second opinion; and to raise a complaint without it affecting your care. Speak to the nurse in charge or email us.

Cookies

We use essential storage to make the site work and remember your choices. With your consent, we also use privacy-friendly analytics and error tracking to see which pages help people and to fix problems. You can change your choice at any time using cookie settings.

Terms of use

This website gives general information about our services. It is not medical advice and does not create a doctor–patient relationship. Always follow the advice of your own doctor or care team.

A request sent through this website is not a confirmed booking until our team contacts you. Prices, hours and insurance arrangements may change; we confirm them in writing before treatment. Images show our unit or are used with permission, and insurer logos are trademarks of their owners. Patient stories are shared only with written consent.

This website does not use AI chat or automated medical advice. These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: readable text sizes, strong colour contrast, keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels. If anything on this site is hard to use, tell us at info@example.com and we'll fix it.
