Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

Nikko Dialysis Center ("we", "us") runs an outpatient haemodialysis centre at [STREET ADDRESS], [AREA], Nairobi, Kenya. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website and when you contact us.

Questions about this policy or your data: info@example.com or [+254 PHONE].

What this policy covers

This policy covers information you give us through this website (booking requests, email sign-ups), and when you call, WhatsApp or email us. Medical records created during your treatment are covered by our separate patient confidentiality notice, which you receive at your first visit.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address (optional).
  • Request details: the services you ask about, who the request is for, preferred shift, contact method and time, travel dates, your current dialysis unit and any message you write.
  • Consent records: the date, time and wording of the consent you gave.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends (such as device type and time zone). If you accept analytics, anonymous usage statistics; we do not use advertising trackers.

We do not ask you to upload medical records through this website. Please don't include detailed medical information in the message box.

Health information

Information about your health is sensitive personal data under the Act. We only process it with your explicit consent (given on our booking form) or where it is necessary to provide your care, and it is handled by staff bound by professional confidentiality.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data or share it for advertising. We share only what is needed with:

  • The Social Health Authority and your insurer, to verify cover, with your consent.
  • Your referring doctor or home dialysis unit, for transfers and holiday sessions.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under written agreements: website hosting (Vercel), email delivery (Resend) and, if you consent, analytics (Plausible) and error monitoring (Sentry).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Transfers outside Kenya

Some of our service providers store data outside Kenya. Where this happens we rely on appropriate safeguards required by the Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations, such as contractual protections and providers with recognised security standards.

How we protect it

The site is served only over HTTPS. Enquiries are sent to a restricted mailbox and accessed only by staff who need them. We use strong passwords and two-factor authentication on our accounts, keep backups, and train staff on confidentiality. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will notify the Data Commissioner and you as the Act requires.

Your rights

Under section 26 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be informed of how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • object to the processing of all or part of your data;
  • correct false or misleading data;
  • delete false, misleading or no-longer-needed data;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing already done);
  • ask for your data in a portable format, where applicable.

To use any right, email info@example.com or ask at reception. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we aim to respond within 14 days. There is no charge in most cases.

If you are in the EU or UK, you have equivalent rights under the GDPR.

Children

Requests about a patient under 18 should be made by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children through this website.

Complaints

Please contact us first so we can put things right. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner: odpc.go.ke.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
