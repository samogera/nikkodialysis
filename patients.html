Between sessions

Small habits, big difference.

General guidance only. Your care team sets your personal fluid and diet limits.

01Watch your fluidsKeep to the daily allowance your team gives you, including tea, soup and fruit.
02Go easy on saltLess salt means less thirst. Season with herbs, lemon and onion instead.
03Protect your accessNo BP cuffs, tight sleeves or heavy bags on your fistula arm. Check the buzz daily.
04Never skip a sessionRunning late or unwell? Call us. We will find another slot rather than lose a treatment.

Get help straight away for chest pain, severe breathlessness, bleeding from your access that won't stop, or if your fistula buzz disappears. Go to the nearest emergency department and call us on [+254 PHONE].

Questions

Frequently asked.

How often will I need dialysis?

Most people need haemodialysis three times a week, for about four hours each time. Your nephrologist sets the exact plan based on your blood results and how you feel.

Does dialysis hurt?

Inserting the needles can sting briefly. Most people feel fine during treatment; some feel tired afterwards. Tell your nurse about cramps or dizziness and they will adjust your session.

Can I eat during my session?

A light snack is usually fine. Ask your nurse first, as eating can lower blood pressure for some people.

Can I keep working while on dialysis?

Many patients do. Choose the morning, midday or evening shift that fits around your job and keep the same chair and time every week.

What if I miss a session?

Call us as soon as you can. Missing treatment lets fluid and waste build up, so we will try to fit you in the same or next day.

Can I dialyse with you while visiting Nairobi?

Yes. Ask your home unit to send your prescription and recent blood results at least two weeks before you travel, and we will reserve your sessions.

Do you accept SHA and private insurance?

Yes. Bring your SHA number and insurance card to your consultation and we confirm your cover and handle pre-authorisation before you start.

How can I pay a balance?

By M-Pesa, Airtel Money, bank transfer or cash. You get a receipt for every session.

Can someone stay with me during treatment?

One companion is welcome for your first sessions. After that it depends on space in the unit.

Can I get a kidney transplant?

Many people on dialysis can be assessed. Your nephrologist will discuss it with you and refer you to a transplant team when appropriate.
