What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (like local storage) are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. Some are essential for a site to work; others help the owner understand how the site is used.

What we use

NamePurposeType · duration
nikko-consentRemembers whether you chose "Accept all" or "Essential only"Essential · until cleared
nikko-checklistRemembers the items you ticked on the first-session checklist (stays on your device)Essential · until cleared
Plausible AnalyticsAnonymous page-view statistics. Uses no cookies and collects no personal dataAnalytics · only if accepted
SentryReports technical errors so we can fix themAnalytics · only if accepted

We do not use advertising or social-media tracking cookies. The embedded map (if shown) is provided by Google and may set its own cookies; see Google's privacy policy.

Your choice

Analytics and error tracking load only after you choose "Accept all" in the cookie notice. You can change your mind at any time:

You can also clear or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may stop some features (like remembering your choice) from working.
