Ways to pay

Three routes. One clear bill.

01

Social Health Authority

SHA covers haemodialysis for registered members under its chronic care benefit. Bring your ID and SHA number and we verify eligibility and your current session benefit on the day.

02

Private insurance

We request pre-authorisation directly from your insurer and can combine private cover with SHA to reduce what you pay yourself.

03

Self-pay & corporate

Per-session price: [KES PRICE]. Pay by M-Pesa, Airtel Money, bank transfer or cash. Employers and embassies can open a corporate account.

How approval works

What happens with your cover.

If you need dialysis urgently, we'll talk you through the options at your consultation.

  1. 01Share your detailsID, SHA number and insurance card, at your consultation or by WhatsApp.
  2. 02We verify & pre-authoriseOur billing team checks eligibility with SHA and requests approval from your insurer.
  3. 03You get a written estimateWhat's covered, what isn't, and any balance per session, before treatment starts.
  4. 04Receipt for every sessionMonthly statements to you, your insurer or your employer on request.
